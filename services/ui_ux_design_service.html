FAQ

Common questions about UI/UX design

Answers to the questions we hear most from product teams considering a UI/UX engagement.

Have a different question? Let's talk →
How is UI/UX design different from product design?

UI/UX design focuses on improving existing interfaces — making them clearer, easier to use, and more effective. Product design is broader: it covers designing a new product from early concepts through launch-ready screens. If your product already exists but users are struggling with it, UI/UX design is the right starting point.

What does a typical UI/UX project look like?

Most projects start with a UX audit — we review your product, map the user journeys, and identify the biggest friction points. From there, we redesign the key screens and flows in Figma, iterating with your team. We deliver high-fidelity designs, interactive prototypes, and a developer-ready handoff package.

How long does a UI/UX redesign usually take?

It depends on scope. A focused redesign of a few key screens or flows can take 2–4 weeks. A more comprehensive redesign — covering onboarding, core workflows, and a dashboard — typically runs 6–10 weeks. We'll scope the timeline together during our initial call based on your specific situation.

Do you handle frontend development too?

Yes. We design in Figma and build in Angular, so the same team can take a project from design through to working frontend code. This reduces handoff problems and means the final product matches the design more closely. If your stack uses a different framework, we deliver detailed design specs and a structured handoff instead.

What if I only need a UX audit, not a full redesign?

That's a perfectly good starting point. A UX audit gives you a clear picture of what's working and what isn't, with prioritized recommendations your team can act on. Many clients start with an audit and then decide which parts to redesign based on the findings.

How do you work with our existing development team?

We design with implementation in mind from day one. During the project, we share progress through Figma so your developers can follow along. At handoff, we provide organized Figma files with component specs, spacing, states, and responsive breakpoints — everything your engineering team needs to build accurately without guessing.
